Written by the coach. Done by you.

PTrainr is a training app for coaches and the people they train, on the web, Android and iOS. One plan, two sides of it, every set accounted for.

WebAndroidiOS19 languagesPrivate pilot

The coach writes it once.

A workout is built from a library of exercises and assigned to one client or several. Each client gets a page: what is coming up, what was done, every set they logged. Changing a workout later never rewrites what somebody already did.

The client ticks it off, one-handed.

Targets arrive filled in, in the coach's blue. A tick logs the set as written; a different number replaces it in graphite. Every set saves as it is ticked, and with no signal they wait on the phone and go up in order later.

Meals, weight, and a line to the coach.

Food from a search, a typed line or a barcode. Measurements that keep the unit they were entered in. Messages between one client and one coach. All of it private until the client shares it, per coach and per category.

One app, built three times, kept the same.

The website is the source of truth; Android (Kotlin, Compose) and iOS (SwiftUI) copy it screen for screen, and a check fails the build when a tab, a word or a colour drifts. No framework on the web, no build step, sign-in and passkeys written by hand.

It used to look like every dashboard.

In October the whole app was photographed, 990 screenshots, and audited. Three worlds were drawn as working mock-ups and one was picked: a paper training log, with two inks. Blue is always the coach. Graphite is always the client.
